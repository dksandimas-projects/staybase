The spark inn Story

Founded on the principles of warm minimalism, spark inn began as a vision to harmonize Bohol's raw natural beauty with contemporary boutique luxury. We believed that true comfort isn't found in excess, but in the intentionality of every detail.

From our hand-carved local timber accents to the curated sensory journey of our dining experiences, every corner of spark inn is a chapter in our narrative of understated elegance and heartfelt hospitality.

We aren't just a destination; we are a home for the soul, where the whispers of the ocean meet the quiet confidence of modern design.
